# unwind

List any Solana token, with leverage.

1. Anyone can open a perpetual market on a token with an on-chain pool behind it: Raydium CLMM or Meteora DLMM.
2. Orders clear together in batch auctions, one price per batch.
3. Backers post first-loss capital to a market and earn 50% of its LP fees.
4. Live on Solana devnet.
